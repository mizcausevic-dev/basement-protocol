## Import steps

1. Medium does not import Markdown files directly. Either use Medium's "Import a story" with the live article URL, or paste the rendered article: open `article.html`, select the article body, copy, and paste into a new Medium story.
2. Upload `article/medium-hero-Cursor.jpg` as the first image and set it as the featured image.
3. Medium renders tables poorly. Convert the three tables (pack families, step symbols, validation checks) into short lists, or upload them as images.
4. Keep the key-takeaways block near the top. It answers the main search intent in the first screen.
5. Check that every deep link (`#BP01` to `#BP50`) opens the matching pattern in the live lab after the site is deployed.
